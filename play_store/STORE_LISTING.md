### ✨ KEY FEATURES

#### 🌌 1. Ultra-HD 4K & True OLED/AMOLED Wallpapers
Every static wallpaper in our curated catalog is hand-selected and optimized in modern WebP format. Enjoy pure pitch-black backgrounds designed specifically for AMOLED displays to maximize battery life while highlighting every star, ridge, and neon accent.

#### 🎬 2. Native Live Video Wallpaper Engine
Experience fluid, high-frame-rate looping video wallpapers without the battery drain. Aura's native live wallpaper engine is engineered with strict Android lifecycle awareness:
- **Instant Pause on Hidden:** The video decoder stops immediately when your screen turns off or when you open another app.
- **Zero Background Audio:** All live wallpapers are strictly visual without unexpected audio tracks or wake locks.
- **Battery-Saver Mode:** Automatically pauses or reduces rendering during system low-battery states.

#### ⚡ 3. Fast, Fluid & Lightweight
No bloat, no unnecessary background services, and zero intrusive popups. Aura uses cutting-edge Jetpack Compose and hardware-accelerated image decoding to ensure instant thumbnail loading, smooth scrolling, and negligible memory consumption.

#### 🔍 4. Curated Taxonomies & Instant Discovery
Explore over 25 meticulously organized categories:
- **AMOLED & Dark:** True #000000 black contrasts.
- **Minimal:** Clean lines, generous negative space, and architectural simplicity.
- **Space & Cosmos:** Distant nebulas, star clusters, and orbital views.
- **Mountains & Landscapes:** Snow-dusted peaks, alpine ridges, and golden-hour valleys.

- **Cars, Bikes, Technology, Architecture, and more.**

#### 📲 5. Native System Wallpaper Apply Flow
Apply static wallpapers directly to your **Home Screen**, **Lock Screen**, or **Both Screens** with a single tap. Live wallpapers seamlessly hook into Android's native WallpaperService preview for 100% manufacturer compatibility across Samsung One UI, Google Pixel, OnePlus OxygenOS, Xiaomi HyperOS, and Motorola devices.

#### 📥 6. Offline Library & Smart Storage Management
Save your favorite wallpapers for offline enjoyment. Easily inspect disk cache usage and manage downloaded files right from the in-app settings with one-tap cleanup.
